Delete your MERG account

App: MERG: Carousel & Story Maker (Android, package com.verenou.veyo)
Developer: MERG
Last updated: 27 October 2026

You can use MERG without an account. If you signed in with Google, you can ask us to delete your MERG account and the data linked to it at any time. You don't need to have the app installed to do this.

How to request deletion

By email (works for everyone):

  1. From the email address of the Google account you used to sign in to MERG, send an email to developer.veyo@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject "Delete my MERG account". You don't need to write anything else.
  3. We'll confirm by email when your account has been deleted. If you email from a different address, we may ask you to confirm the request from the address linked to your account, so we know it's really you.

In the app: if your version of MERG has a delete account option in its settings, you can use that instead. The email route above always works.

What we delete

Within 30 days of your request, we permanently delete your account from Firebase Authentication, including:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your profile photo
  • your MERG (Firebase) user ID

What we keep, and for how long

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are not stored with your name or email. They are deleted automatically after up to 90 days.
  • Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) are not stored with your name or email. They are deleted automatically after up to 14 months.
  • Your deletion request email and our reply are kept for up to 24 months, so we can show that we handled your request, then deleted.

If you also want us to delete crash or analytics data, say so in your email and we will delete what we can identify as yours.

Data on your phone

Your photos, videos and projects are stored only on your phone, never on our servers, so deleting your account doesn't touch them. To remove them, delete them in the app, clear MERG's app data in Android settings, or uninstall MERG.

Questions
