Veyo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026
Applies to: the Veyo: Carousel & Story Maker Android app (package com.verenou.veyo)

This policy explains what data Veyo handles, why, and what you can do about it. We've tried to keep it short and plain. If something is unclear, email us at developer.veyo@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

Veyo is made by Veyo ("we", "us"). We are based in India. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), Veyo is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described here. We also follow the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (IT Rules 2011). Veyo is available worldwide, so where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to you, Veyo is the controller of your personal data.

  • Contact: developer.veyo@gmail.com
  • Website: https://veyo-app.github.io/
  • Grievance contact: see section 13

2. The short version

  • You can use Veyo without an account (guest mode).
  • The photos and videos you pick stay on your phone. We do not upload them.
  • Your projects are saved on your phone, not on our servers.
  • If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile photo and a user ID.
  • We use Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase to find crashes and understand how the app is used.
  • Veyo has no ads. We do not sell your data.
  • You can ask us to delete your account at any time. See https://veyo-app.github.io/delete-account/.

3. What we collect and why

Our grounds for using your data. For users in India, we process personal data on the basis of your consent, which you give by using the app and, where it applies, by signing in, or for other legitimate uses allowed under the DPDP Act. You can withdraw consent at any time (see section 7). For users in the EU, EEA or UK, the GDPR legal basis is listed under each item below.

We do not collect sensitive personal data or information as defined in the IT Rules 2011, such as passwords, financial details, health data or biometric data. Google handles your Google sign-in; we never see your Google password.

3.1 Your photos, videos and projects

When you add photos or videos, Veyo uses the Android system photo picker. You choose exactly which files Veyo can see. Veyo edits those files on your phone. They are not uploaded to us or anyone else.

Your projects (your edits, layouts and settings) are saved in a local database on your phone. We cannot see them. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, they are gone.

When you share to Instagram or another app, the exported file goes to the app you choose through Android's share feature. From there, that app's own privacy policy applies.

3.2 Account data (only if you sign in)

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google, we use Firebase Authentication, a Google service, and receive:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your profile photo
  • a Firebase user ID (a random ID that identifies your account)

Firebase Authentication also processes your IP address during sign-in to protect against abuse.

Why: to create and run your account and show you which account you are signed in with.
Legal basis (EU/UK users): performance of a contract (providing the account you asked for), GDPR Art. 6(1)(b).

3.3 Crash reports and diagnostics

We use Firebase Crashlytics. When the app crashes or hits a serious error, it sends a report that may include:

  • what went wrong (crash logs and technical state of the app at the time)
  • device details such as model, Android version and app version
  • an installation ID that lets us count how many devices are affected

Why: to find and fix bugs and keep the app stable.
Legal basis (EU/UK users): our legitimate interest in providing a working, secure app, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f). You can object at any time (see section 7).

3.4 Usage analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how people use Veyo, for example, which screens people open, which features they use and how often. This includes:

  • app interactions (events like opening the editor or exporting a post)
  • device and app details (model, Android version, language, app version)
  • identifiers used by Firebase, such as an app instance ID
  • a rough idea of your country or region, which Google works out from your IP address

We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not use it to show you ads or build advertising profiles.

Why: to see what works, what doesn't, and where to improve the app.
Legal basis (EU/UK users): our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the app, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f), or your consent, Art. 6(1)(a), where the law requires consent. You can object at any time, or withdraw any consent you gave, by emailing us (see section 7).

3.5 Templates and content downloads

Templates, stickers, frames and animations are downloaded from our content feed. Today the feed is hosted on GitHub Pages (run by GitHub, Inc.). We may move it to another provider, such as Firebase, later.

To send you these files, the hosting service receives standard technical information that every web request includes, such as your IP address and device type. We use these requests only to deliver content to the app.

Why: to deliver templates to the app.
Legal basis (EU/UK users): performance of a contract, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), and our legitimate interest in delivering content reliably, Art. 6(1)(f).

3.6 Emails you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and help you.

Legal basis (EU/UK users): legitimate interest in answering you, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f).

4. What we don't do

  • We don't upload your photos or videos.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't sell your personal data or share it for advertising.
  • We don't access your contacts, location (GPS), camera or microphone.
  • On modern Android versions, the system photo picker gives Veyo only the files you choose.

If Veyo is sold or transferred to someone else, your data may move with it. We will tell you before that happens.

Where your data is processed

We are based in India, but our service providers, Google and GitHub, store and process data on servers in the United States and other countries. Data you share with us may therefore be processed outside India and outside the country where you live. We only use providers that apply strong security measures, and we will not transfer personal data to any country that the Government of India restricts under the DPDP Act.

For users in the EU, EEA or UK, transfers to these providers rely on safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK extension) and Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Your rights

Everyone can email us to ask what data we hold about them, or to correct or delete it.

If you are in India, under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • get a summary of the personal data we process about you and how we process it
  • have your data corrected, completed, updated or erased
  • withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect processing that happened before.
  • have your grievances addressed (see section 13)
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to do so

If you are in the EU, EEA or UK, under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • correct data that is wrong
  • delete your data
  • restrict how we use it
  • object to uses based on legitimate interest, like crash reporting and analytics
  • data portability, getting your data in a common format
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent. This does not affect what happened before.

To use any of these rights, email developer.veyo@gmail.com. We will reply within one month. We may need to check that the request really comes from you.

If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to a data protection authority. In India, once you have used our grievance process in section 13, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India. In the EU or EEA, you can contact the data protection authority in your country. In the UK, that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), https://ico.org.uk.

8. Deleting your account and data

You can ask us to delete your account at any time:

  1. By email: send an email to developer.veyo@gmail.com from the Google account email you use with Veyo, with the subject "Delete my Veyo account".
  2. In the app: if your version of Veyo includes a delete account option in its settings, you can use that instead.

Full steps are on our account deletion page: https://veyo-app.github.io/delete-account/.

When you delete your account, we delete your name, email address, profile photo and user ID from Firebase Authentication within 30 days. Crash reports and analytics data are kept only for the periods in section 6 and are then deleted automatically. If you also want us to delete crash or analytics data, say so in your email and we will delete what we can identify as yours.

Your projects live on your phone. To remove them, delete them in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall Veyo.

9. Security

Data sent between the app and Firebase or our content feed is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Google protects stored data with its own security measures. No system is perfectly secure. We try to collect as little as we can, because that is the best protection there is.

10. Children

Veyo is not directed at children. You must be at least 13 to use Veyo. Under Indian law, a child is anyone under 18, and their personal data may only be processed with the verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian. If you are in India and under 18, please use Veyo only with your parent's or guardian's permission, and do not sign in. If you believe a child has given us personal data without the consent the law requires, email developer.veyo@gmail.com and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

When Veyo changes, this policy may change too. For example, we plan to offer subscriptions in a later version, which will mean new data such as purchase records handled through Google Play. We will update this page and change the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
